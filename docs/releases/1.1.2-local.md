# Capri 1.1.2 local: desktop roaming

Changes in this fork on top of Capri 1.1.2, following the desktop-roaming plan.
They change nothing until **Let Capri move around on her own** is on, and
windows are only used once **Let Capri sit on app windows** is on too.

## Main changes

- **Sit on app windows** (off by default) lets roaming use open app windows
  without screen observation. Only window positions and sizes are read: no
  screen pixels, titles or window contents.
- Each move is planned over the surfaces that are really open: top edges,
  top corners (with a mirrored pose on the right), sides near the top,
  undersides and the taskbar line. Edges hidden by a window in front are
  skipped; a free spot in the air is only the last resort.
- Drag and drop can seat Capri on a window's top corners.
- A perch slides, redocks or leaves when its window is covered, snapped or no
  longer leaves room for the pose. A closed, minimized or maximized window
  sends Capri to a planned spot instead of a screen corner.
- Moves along the same edge glide; nearby moves use a quicker, smaller
  teleport; Capri lingers longer on the window you are working in.
- **Settings → Developer → For developers** lists the last 20 roaming decisions
  and the native window follower's status. The follower restarts on its own
  after an unexpected stop.
- Lighter on low-end PCs: the roaming tick does nothing while roaming is off,
  and the teleport tint is no longer built for the arriving pose.

## Where to check

1. With three normal windows, roaming at 30 seconds and windows allowed, watch
   ten moves: mostly edges, corners and the taskbar. Read the developer panel.
2. Maximize the window Capri sits on: she lands on the taskbar or another
   window, never in a screen corner.
3. Snap two windows to the left and right halves: no title bar is covered.
4. Bring another window over her perch: she slides along the edge, or leaves
   within about two seconds.
5. Drop her on both top corners of a window: legs over the side, and the right
   corner mirrored. Check the face and lighting of the mirrored pose.
6. Turn on reduced motion: no glide or short hop.
7. Turn **Let Capri sit on app windows** off: free hops only, as in 1.1.2.

Capri's own settings file gains `avatarRoamWindows`. Official Capri builds do
not know it: after an official update, redeploy this fork before changing any
setting.
